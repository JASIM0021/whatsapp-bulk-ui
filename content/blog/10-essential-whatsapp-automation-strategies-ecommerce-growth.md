For an online store, WhatsApp can do something email rarely does: start a conversation. A customer who gets a personal message about a restock can reply “Do you have it in M?” and buy within minutes.

The difference between that and spam is relevance and respect. These ten automations focus on messages customers are glad to get.

## 1. Restock alerts

When a popular item is back, message the customers who asked about it or bought it before.

> Hi Ananya, the linen shirt you asked about is back in stock in all sizes. Want me to keep one aside for you?

Use a spreadsheet column for the product so each message names the right item.

## 2. Sale and festival announcements

Announce sales a day or two before they start, with one clear offer and a link. Schedule the campaign for a time your customers are free, like early evening, and switch on [AI replies](/blog/build-automated-whatsapp-bot-for-business) before it goes out.

## 3. New collection previews

Send a single image and a short line to your best customers before a launch. Early access feels like a perk, not an advert.

## 4. Answer product questions automatically

Every campaign creates questions: sizes, colours, fabric, stock. Give the AI your product details and policies so it answers in seconds and shares the product link. Hand anything unusual to a person by keyword.

## 5. Delivery and returns questions

“Do you deliver to my pin code?” and “How do I return this?” are perfect for AI. Put your delivery areas, timelines and return policy in the business details.

## 6. Review requests after delivery

A few days after an order arrives, send a short thank-you and ask for a review. Keep it to one sentence and one link, and only ask once.

## 7. Win back lapsed customers

Message customers who haven’t ordered in a few months with something relevant to what they bought before, not a generic discount. Personalisation is what makes this work.

## 8. Follow up on campaign non-replies

For important announcements, send one polite follow-up to people who didn’t reply. NexBotix can write and send this AI follow-up automatically. Stop after one.

## 9. Turn website visitors into WhatsApp conversations

Use a [website chatbot](/blog/transforming-website-leads-ai-chatbots-whatsapp-integration) to answer shoppers on your site, capture their number when they’re interested, and continue the conversation on WhatsApp.

## 10. Order updates through the API

Send order confirmations and shipping updates from your store’s backend with the [WhatsApp API](/docs). For high volumes of transactional messages, use the official WhatsApp Business Cloud API.

## How do I keep customers happy, not annoyed?

| Do | Don’t |
| --- | --- |
| Ask permission at checkout | Message people who never opted in |
| Use their name and purchase | Send the same blast to everyone |
| Keep it to a few lines | Send long, ad-like messages |
| Offer an easy opt-out | Make people report you to stop |
| Answer replies quickly | Leave replies unread for days |
