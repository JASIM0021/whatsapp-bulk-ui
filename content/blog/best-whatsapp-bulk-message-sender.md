“Bulk WhatsApp sender” covers very different tools. Some are free features inside WhatsApp, some send from your own number, and some are enterprise platforms on Meta’s official API. Picking the wrong type is the most common, and most expensive, mistake.

This guide explains the three approaches first, then compares six options. **Prices come from each vendor’s own page, checked on 14 October 2026.** NexBotix is our product; we say where others are the better fit.

## What are the three ways to send bulk WhatsApp messages?

### 1. Broadcast lists in the WhatsApp Business app (free)

The WhatsApp Business app lets you create broadcast lists and send one message to everyone on a list. It costs nothing. The catch: only people who have **saved your number** receive broadcasts, and each list is limited in size (widely reported as 256 contacts). There is no personalisation, scheduling or automation.

**Good for:** a small shop messaging regular customers.

### 2. A sender tool on your own number

These tools connect to your existing WhatsApp number, usually by scanning a QR code like WhatsApp Web, and send personalised messages from a spreadsheet. No Meta approval or per-message fees. The trade-off is risk: your personal or business-app number does the sending, so careless campaigns can get it restricted.

**Good for:** small businesses with warm customer lists who want personalisation, scheduling and AI replies without API costs.

### 3. A WhatsApp Business API platform

Platforms built on Meta’s official WhatsApp Business Platform send approved message templates at scale, with team inboxes, chatbots and integrations. Meta charges **per delivered template message** (marketing, utility, authentication) since 1 July 2025, at rates that vary by country; replies inside the 24-hour customer service window are free ([Meta pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)). The platform adds its subscription on top.

**Good for:** large lists, transactional messages and teams.

## 1. WhatsApp Business app broadcast lists

The simplest option and completely free. Create a list, add contacts and send. Recipients see the message as a normal one-to-one chat.

- **Pros:** free, official, no setup.
- **Cons:** only reaches people who saved your number, small lists, no names, no scheduling, no automation, replies pile up in your phone.
- **Choose it if** you have a few dozen loyal customers and send occasionally.

## How do you pick the right one?

Answer three questions:

1. **How big is your list?** Under a few hundred warm contacts: broadcasts or a sender on your own number. Thousands, regularly: an API platform.
2. **Who handles replies?** Just you: pick a tool with AI replies. A team: pick a shared inbox.
3. **What’s your budget including message fees?** Price your real monthly volume, including Meta’s per-message charges on API platforms.

Whichever you choose, the rules that keep your number safe are the same; read [how to send bulk WhatsApp messages without getting banned](/blog/bulk-whatsapp-messaging-best-practices-avoid-bans) before your first big campaign.
