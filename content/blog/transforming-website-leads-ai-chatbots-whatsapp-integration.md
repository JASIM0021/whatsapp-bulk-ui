Most people who visit your website leave without telling you anything. Some found what they needed. Many had a question, didn’t see a quick way to ask it, and moved on.

An AI chatbot on your website catches those visitors. Pair it with fast WhatsApp follow-up and you have a simple system that turns anonymous traffic into real conversations.

## Why do website visitors leave without contacting you?

Because contacting you is work. A contact form asks them to write a message and wait, maybe a day, for an answer to a simple question. Phone numbers mean a call during office hours. Email feels slow.

A visitor who is comparing three businesses will usually choose the one that answers first. A chat that replies in seconds, at 11 PM on a Sunday, is often that business.

## What does an AI chatbot do differently?

An AI chatbot trained on your own website can:

- **Answer immediately**, using your actual prices, services, delivery areas and policies.
- **Keep the conversation going**, asking a follow-up question the way a good salesperson would.
- **Capture contact details** when the visitor is interested, instead of demanding them up front.
- **Work all night and weekend**, which is when many people browse.

## How do I write a chatbot that converts?

**Open with a useful welcome.** Tell visitors what they can ask: “Hi! Ask me about our menu, delivery areas or catering for events.” It gets far more conversations started than “How can I help?”.

**Answer first, ask second.** Let the bot answer the question, then offer the next step: “Would you like us to send you a quote on WhatsApp? What’s the best number?”

**Keep it short.** Two or three sentences per reply is plenty.

**Give it facts.** Most poor answers come from information missing on your site. A clear pricing page and FAQ make the bot better overnight.

## How do I follow up on WhatsApp?

Captured leads appear on your chatbot leads page. Speed matters here: follow up the same day while the visitor still remembers you.

A good first WhatsApp message is short and specific:

> Hi Priya, this is Asha from Green Leaf Catering. You asked about catering for 40 people on the 20th. We have that date free. Would a quick call tomorrow help? Here’s a link to pick a time: …

It references what they asked, gives one next step and makes booking easy. If you use [NexBotix Calendar](/ai-agents/calendar), the booking link adds a Google Meet room automatically.

## What should I measure?

| Metric | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Chats started | Whether visitors notice and trust the widget |
| Leads captured | Whether the bot moves interested people to share details |
| Follow-up replies | Whether your WhatsApp message lands |
| Bookings or sales | Whether the whole system works |

Read the chat transcripts too. The questions visitors ask are free research: if many ask the same thing, put the answer on your website.
