WATI is one of the best-known WhatsApp Business API platforms, especially in India. It does a lot well: a shared team inbox, broadcasts, chatbots and integrations with tools like HubSpot and Salesforce.

But it isn’t the right fit for everyone. Some teams find the per-user add-ons add up, some need something simpler, and some want AI to do more of the work. This guide compares five alternatives. **Every price below comes from the vendor’s own pricing page or app listing, checked on 14 October 2026.** Where a vendor doesn’t publish a number, we say so instead of guessing.

*Disclosure: NexBotix is our product. We’ve included it because it’s a genuine alternative for some readers, and we say clearly where the others are the better choice.*

## What does WATI offer, and why do people look elsewhere?

WATI’s pricing page lists three plans: **Growth**, **Pro** and **Business**. According to that page:

- **Growth** includes 3 users, 15,000 broadcasts a month, 1,000 automation triggers and 2 commerce or CRM integrations.
- **Pro** includes 5 users, with extra users at **$24 per user per month**, unlimited broadcasts, 2,000 automation triggers and HubSpot.
- **Business** includes 5 users, with extra users at **$69 per user per month**, Salesforce and higher limits.
- A Shopify integration is a **$4.99 per month** add-on, and WhatsApp message charges are deducted from prepaid credits based on WATI’s rate card.

The base plan prices weren’t displayed in a form we could verify, so check them on [WATI’s pricing page](https://www.wati.io/pricing/).

The reasons teams look elsewhere usually come down to:

1. **Seat costs** once a team grows beyond the included users.
2. **Feature tiers**: some automation and integrations sit in higher plans.
3. **Fit**: a solo founder or small shop may not need a full team inbox.

## What should you compare before switching?

| Factor | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Pricing model | Per user, per plan, per active contact or per credit changes your bill as you grow |
| WhatsApp message fees | Meta charges per delivered template message; check the provider’s rate card and any markup |
| Team inbox | Essential for support teams, unnecessary for a solo owner |
| Automation and AI | Chatbot builders, AI agents and how many AI replies are included |
| Integrations | Shopify, CRM and API access for your stack |
| Channels | WhatsApp only, or Instagram and web chat too |

A quick note on Meta’s fees, because they apply to every API-based tool. Since 1 July 2025 Meta charges **per message** rather than per conversation. Marketing, utility and authentication **template** messages are charged on delivery, at rates that depend on the recipient’s country. Non-template replies inside an open customer service window are free, and so are messages within the 72-hour free entry point window after a click-to-WhatsApp ad ([Meta’s pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)).

## 1. AiSensy

AiSensy is an India-focused WhatsApp marketing platform. Its pricing page describes an **Unlimited Users** subscription with “no limit on the number of Monthly Active Users”, billed monthly, quarterly (5% off) or yearly (10% off). The base subscription price wasn’t shown on the page when we checked.

Published add-ons include a **drag-and-drop chatbot builder at ₹2,500 a month** (₹2,250 billed annually), with 5 chatbots and Shopify chatbot integration, and a **WhatsApp AI Agent builder at ₹1,350 a month** (₹1,215 annually) with 1,000 AI messages a month. Message charges are prepaid credits; for Indian numbers the page lists marketing messages at **₹1.09** and utility and authentication at **₹0.145** each, with the first 1,000 service messages per number per month free.

- **Strengths:** unlimited users, transparent per-message rates for India, strong marketing focus.
- **Watch out for:** chatbot and AI agent builders are paid add-ons, so the total depends on what you add.
- **Best for:** marketing teams in India sending campaigns at volume.

Source: [AiSensy pricing](https://aisensy.com/pricing).

## 2. Interakt

Interakt is popular with Shopify stores. Its own [Shopify app listing](https://apps.shopify.com/interakt-marketing) shows four plans billed in USD, each with a **14-day free trial**:

| Plan | Monthly | Annual |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $21 | $180/year |
| Growth | $41 | $296/year |
| Advanced | $51 | $468/year |
| Premier | $99 | $839/year |

Starter covers API setup, automated notifications, a shared team inbox and a WhatsApp widget. Growth adds analytics, a basic chatbot and Instagram automation. Advanced adds an advanced chatbot and auto-routing. Premier adds three WhatsApp AI agents and cart reminders. WhatsApp conversation charges are extra.

- **Strengths:** clear published pricing, good Shopify fit, low entry price.
- **Watch out for:** advanced chatbots and AI agents sit in the higher tiers.
- **Best for:** Shopify and D2C brands.

## 3. Gallabox

Gallabox publishes its prices clearly on its [pricing page](https://gallabox.com/pricing):

| Plan | Quarterly billing | Annual billing | Users | AI credits/mo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | ₹2,999/mo | ₹2,399/mo | 3 | 500 |
| Essential | ₹6,999/mo | ₹5,599/mo | 6 | 2,000 |
| Advanced | ₹16,999/mo | ₹13,599/mo | 10 | 8,000 |

Every plan includes WhatsApp, Instagram and a web chat widget, chatbot flows and 20+ integrations. Essential adds AI agents on WhatsApp and Instagram, a full API with two-way CRM integrations and free onboarding. Advanced adds AI voice and appointment booking management. Message fees are billed separately.

- **Strengths:** transparent pricing, multichannel inbox, strong AI and CRM features in higher tiers.
- **Watch out for:** user caps per plan, and the jump between tiers is significant.
- **Best for:** support and sales teams that live in a shared inbox.

## 4. DoubleTick

DoubleTick offers **Starter**, **Pro** and **Enterprise** plans. Its [pricing page](https://doubletick.io/pricing) says AI agents are built into every plan, with 24x7 chat support; Pro adds a shared account manager and Enterprise a dedicated one. Prices weren’t shown on the page when we checked, and per-message costs vary by country.

- **Strengths:** AI agents included rather than sold separately; account management on higher plans.
- **Watch out for:** you’ll need a quote or sign-up to see prices.
- **Best for:** small sales teams that want AI help without add-ons.

## 5. NexBotix (our product)

NexBotix takes a different approach. Instead of a team inbox, it gives you **AI employees**, each owning a job. The WhatsApp employee, [Nexa](/ai-agents/whatsapp), sends personalised campaigns from Excel or CSV, schedules them, follows up with people who didn’t reply and answers replies with AI, handing chats to a person by keyword.

You can send from your **existing WhatsApp number** connected by QR code, or use the **official WhatsApp Business Cloud API**. Plans start at **₹399 a month for 2,500 credits** (Starter), with Growth at ₹1,199 for 8,000 credits; every plan includes every AI employee, and the free trial gives 100 credits with no card ([pricing](/pricing)). The same account includes other AI employees for leads, email, website chat and booking.

- **Strengths:** low entry price, AI that handles the whole campaign and its replies, plus leads, email and website chat in one plan.
- **Watch out for:** it isn’t built around a large multi-agent shared inbox or deep CRM integrations. If that’s what you need, Gallabox or WATI are a better fit. Sending from a personal number also carries more risk than the official API; see our [guide to bulk messaging without bans](/blog/bulk-whatsapp-messaging-best-practices-avoid-bans).
- **Best for:** small businesses and solo founders who want campaigns and replies handled without a team.

## Which WATI alternative should you choose?

- **You run a support team in a shared inbox with a CRM:** Gallabox, or stay on WATI.
- **You sell on Shopify and want low, published prices:** Interakt.
- **You send high-volume marketing in India and want unlimited users:** AiSensy.
- **You want AI agents included in every plan:** DoubleTick.
- **You’re a small business that wants AI to send and reply for you:** NexBotix.

## How do you switch from WATI?

1. **List what you use:** templates, automations, integrations, number of users and monthly message volume.
2. **Price it on two or three alternatives** using your real volume, including Meta’s message fees.
3. **Ask about number migration.** Numbers on the WhatsApp Business Platform can usually move between providers; ask the new provider to handle it and confirm what happens to templates and chat history.
4. **Rebuild your top automations first**, test them on your own number, then move your campaigns.
5. **Run both for a short overlap** if you can, so customers never hit a dead end.
